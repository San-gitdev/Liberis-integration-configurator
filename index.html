Step 04 / Products & Rules
What to offer and who qualifies
Enable products and configure eligibility. Your platform-level rules layer on top of Liberis underwriting — you can restrict access but cannot override Liberis credit decisions.
Products
BCA
Business Cash Advance
Revenue-based advance repaid as a % of daily card takings. No fixed term — repayment flexes with revenue.
FLEXI
Flexi Loan
Fixed-schedule term loan. Predictable repayments for merchants who prefer certainty over flexibility.
RENEWAL
Renewals / Top-ups
Re-offer eligible returning merchants using liberis_id from their original deal. Skips re-registration.
Eligibility & Repayment
Whitelist / Blocklist
Merchant Whitelist
Specific merchant IDs always shown financing regardless of eligibility rules.
Merchant Blocklist
Specific merchant IDs explicitly excluded from all financing.
